### Prompt final

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape editorial website image,
1536x1024, for an explicitly fictional Senegal construction portfolio demo.
Primary request: a warm, beautifully composed realistic illustration of a modest
single-storey family house under construction in a Senegalese residential setting.
Concrete block masonry rising neatly from a foundation, unplastered walls, openings
for doors and windows, tidy piles of sand and dark aggregate in foreground,
authentic sandy earth and a few small trees, neighboring modest homes in soft
background. Show the project mid-construction, not a finished luxury villa.
Composition: wide eye-level three-quarter view, house and material piles fill
frame, strong foreground texture and architectural depth. Lighting: bright
late-afternoon golden sunlight, inviting and optimistic, natural colors, pale
warm stone, golden sand, restrained amber accents, readable shadows, not dark or
apocalyptic. Fine real-world material textures, plausible building geometry.
No workers or identifiable people, no logos, no signs, no text, no watermark.
This is synthetic demonstration imagery, not a photo of an actual LAMACO project.
Return the generated image and saved file path.
