# Generated visual assets

## Usage in the confirmed catalogue

The catalogue now follows the 12 references confirmed by LAMACO (see
`docs/PRODUCT.md`). Basalt, limestone and silex family images illustrate all their
calibres; they are not to scale. The sand image is shared by Sable de Dakar and
Sable de Tasseb and does not document their actual appearance or origin.

`material-gravel.png` and `material-concrete.png` are retained as unused assets.
They no longer illustrate independent offers: the confirmed catalogue sells
aggregates under local concrete-related names, not a confirmed ready-mixed
concrete product. Do not restore these offers based on the images alone.

## Material catalogue additions

Mode: built-in `imagegen`. Final workspace assets and exact prompts:

- `src/assets/images/material-gravel.png`: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: construction-material catalogue photograph, illustrative, not a documentary photo of a named supplier. Primary request: close-up frame-filling pile of construction gravel, small irregular naturally weathered aggregate stones, gray and muted beige, realistic dusty granular texture. Composition: square frame, oblique close view, varied small stones, no horizon, material fills entire frame. Lighting: subdued natural directional light, earthy industrial aesthetic, detailed sharp textures and soft shadows. Constraints: gravel only, no text, no logo, no people, no plants, no tools, no container, no watermark.
- `src/assets/images/material-concrete.png`: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: construction-material catalogue photograph, illustrative, not a documentary photo of a named supplier. Primary request: close-up frame-filling freshly mixed gray concrete, thick rough cohesive wet cement paste with small aggregate partly embedded, showing realistic uneven ridges and subtle moist highlights. Composition: square frame, oblique macro view, concrete fills the entire frame, no horizon. Lighting: subdued natural directional light, premium earthy industrial material photography, neutral gray, detailed physically believable texture. Constraints: concrete only, no text, no logo, no people, no tools, no container edges, no watermark, not a polished dry floor, not a rock pile, not shiny liquid.

## Existing visuals

These raster images are decorative website visuals generated with the built-in
OpenAI image generation tool. They are not documentary photographs of LAMACO,
its team, depot, equipment or projects. The UI alt text identifies them as
illustrations.

Prompt set used for the final assets:

- `hero-quarry-v2.png`: edited cinematic ultra-wide West African quarry at dusk; an
  unbranded tracked excavator on the far left, an unbranded dump truck on the
  far right, a large excavator bucket entering from the upper edge and pouring
  sand and gravel at center, dark central copy space, warm
  amber dust; no people, text, logos, signage or watermark.
- `material-basalt.png`: close-up, frame-filling pile of rough black basalt
  aggregate, low-key directional light; no container, people, text or branding.
- `material-limestone.png`: close-up, frame-filling pile of pale beige crushed
  limestone, realistic dusty texture and warm studio light; no text or branding.
- `material-silex.png`: close-up pile of flint/silex in charcoal, cream and
  ochre tones with realistic mineral texture; no text or branding.
- `material-sand.png`: macro close-up, frame-filling mound of dry golden-beige
  construction sand with varied, angular mineral grains and warm directional
  light; no tools, people, text, branding or watermark.
- `excavator.png`: complete unbranded yellow hydraulic tracked excavator on a
  pale technical studio background, three-quarter side view; no people or text.
- `process-quarry.png`: ultra-wide West African quarry delivery scene at sunset,
  one unbranded dump truck on the lower-left road, aggregate piles and dark copy
  space; no people, text, logos or watermark.
